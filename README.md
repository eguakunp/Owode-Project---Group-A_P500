# Overview of Owode Commerce

[Owode Commerce](https://owode.co/) is an e-commerce infrastructure platform built specifically for small businesses, creators, student entrepreneurs, and social sellers in Nigeria and across the African market. It aims to streamline informal, conversation-driven online commerce by transforming scattered social media interactions into structured business operations.

## Core Problem Solved
In emerging markets, millions of sellers rely heavily on channels like WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook to conduct business. However, managing orders, processing payments, arranging deliveries, and answering repetitive buyer questions through manual chats is highly fragmented and inefficient. Owode Commerce addresses this friction by providing tools that bring structure, trust, and clarity to social selling.

## Key Features & Offerings
* **Digital Storefronts:** Sellers can quickly set up a professional digital storefront to showcase their physical or digital products.
* **Structured Product Links:** Instead of handling repetitive inquiries manually, sellers can share dedicated, organized links directly to social channels or WhatsApp catalogs.
* **Integrated Order & Payment Management:** Orders and online payments are centralized in a single dashboard, with seamless payouts directly to the seller's bank account.
* **Seller-Managed Delivery (SMD):** A flexible delivery system designed around local ecosystem logistics, allowing sellers to handle fulfillment their own way while keeping tracking organized.
* **Owode Campus Marketplace:** Tailored discovery experiences where students and creators can buy and sell products directly within their campus communities.
* **Owode BI (Business Intelligence):** An intelligence layer providing data insights on overall business health, revenue, traffic sources, product performance, and customer behavior.
